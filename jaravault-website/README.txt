JARAVAULT – THREE-PAGE AFRICAN GROCERY WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: Home/storefront page
- about.html: About JaraVault page
- contact.html: Contact page and enquiry form
- style.css: Shared colourful African-inspired styling
- script.js: Mobile navigation, dynamic copyright year and email form helper

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the jaravault-website folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.
4. Use the navigation links to visit Our Story and Contact.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. In contact.html, replace:
   ADD YOUR BUSINESS EMAIL
   ADD YOUR UK PHONE NUMBER
   Add your actual shop address and opening hours.
2. In script.js, replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with your real business email.
3. Replace any sample images or text with your own product and store photographs/details.
4. The contact form currently opens the visitor's email app. It is not a server-backed form.
5. Images and Google Fonts are loaded from external services, so an internet connection is needed to display them.

This is a simple business showcase, not an online shop. It does not include checkout, customer accounts or online payments.
